QUANTUM DUAL V34 LINUX
Release: 2026.08.31-R11.1
Branch: feature/v34-dual-market-engine

Strategy: R11 Calibrated Sweep/Reclaim/MSS + Pending Retest. M5 handles structure/setup, M15 provides aligned directional bias, and M1 is not used for signal generation. Every Binance USD-M USDT perpetual is discovered dynamically from the currently tradable exchange universe, filtered for liquidity, calibrated independently across the R11 parameter grid, and must survive validation/holdout plus a separate external period after simulated costs before it can enter the executable whitelist. Entry waits for the calibrated M5 retest. Binance symbol filters and actual per-symbol minimum notional/step size are respected; there is no hard-coded universal $20 minimum.

R11.1 safety hardening: the Reversal Guard no longer has a fixed 48-coin universe. It watches every OPEN Crypto position in the current execution mode, so a position cannot be left unprotected simply because it came from a broader scanner universe. Reversal score (M15 EMA50/200 + M5 EMA9/21 + RSI + MACD + adverse volume/candle + microstructure) is observational/advisory only. It is recorded in trade metadata and can generate Telegram warnings, but it does NOT close a position early and does NOT veto a valid R11 entry. The only extra automatic close is a structural-SL fail-safe: if Mark Price has already crossed the trade's validated R11 stop while the trade is still OPEN, the guard requests an emergency reduce-only close and blocks immediate re-entry for 4 hours. PAPER/TESTNET/REAL reversal blocks are persisted separately. Binance reconciliation is also isolated by execution mode and preserves EMERGENCY_RISK/REVERSAL close reasons.

Why the legacy reversal rules were rejected: an external Binance Data Vision benchmark on the current window showed that copying the proposed rules literally (ROE <= -5%, score >=50 close, score 30-49 + ROE<-3 close) worsened all 3 comparable R11 models: average net-return delta -0.569 percentage points and average win-rate delta -34.46 percentage points. The pre-entry reversal-score veto was also rejected: threshold 30 blocked 4 trades and all 4 were winners; threshold 40 blocked 2 and both were winners; thresholds 50-60 blocked 1 and it was a winner. Therefore these thresholds are retained only as diagnostics/warnings, not as strategy-changing exits or admission filters.

Benchmark interpretation: on the 2026-08-28 historical benchmark, LINKUSDT and ARBUSDT passed the full external gate with 69.23% and 72.73% external WR respectively. Those figures are evidence for that historical window, not a permanent expected win rate. A 2026-08-31 revalidation produced materially different model readiness/performance, confirming that qualification is regime-dependent. Production must use the current dynamically refreshed whitelist and PAPER evidence rather than assuming a previously qualified symbol remains profitable.

Forex remains SIGNAL_ONLY: currencies + XAUUSD, per-instrument R11 calibration, BUY LIMIT / SELL LIMIT Telegram signals before retest, 3 M5-bar expiry for unfilled signals, and WIN/LOSS tracking only after the LIMIT was actually filled. Default polling is 5 minutes. Historical Binance backtest calibrates on the 21 days preceding the selected range so the selected range remains out-of-sample and includes simulated costs.
