QUANTUM COMMODITIES R12 LINUX
Release: 2026.09.02-R12
Branch: feature/v34-dual-market-engine

Markets: ONLY XAUUSD and CRUDE OIL.
- XAUUSD is executed as XAUUSDT on Binance USD-M Futures. Directions: BUY and SELL.
- CRUDE OIL is executed as CLUSDT on Aster Perpetuals. Direction: BUY ONLY. SELL is hard-disabled in strategy and execution code.

Strategy: dedicated spread-aware scalper. Context is 1-minute EMA9/EMA21 + RSI14 + ATR14. Trigger is synthetic 30-second microstructure built from aggregated trades, including taker buy/sell imbalance, pullback and displacement/break confirmation. M1 is context; 30s is entry timing.

Cost gate: every entry uses real bid/ask spread. Estimated round-trip cost = spread + two taker fees + two slippage allowances. Expected gross TP must be at least 2.5x estimated round-trip cost. PAPER fills long entries at ask plus estimated slippage and long exits at bid minus estimated slippage; shorts use the inverse. This prevents micro-TPs whose gross movement is consumed by spread and fees.

Risk: strict 1% margin target per trade. Margin is never increased to satisfy minimum notional. Default requested leverage is 10x; XAU TradFi leverage is capped at 10x in R12. One open position per symbol and mode. Default max holding time is 180 seconds.

Safety: APP_MODE defaults to PAPER. REAL commodity execution is additionally locked unless COMMODITY_ALLOW_REAL=true. Aster private credentials are not required for PAPER/public CLUSDT data and must stay only in the user's local .env when REAL is intentionally enabled. Legacy Crypto R11 and Twelve Data Forex scanners are not started by the R12 Linux backend.

UI: dedicated two-column dashboard: XAUUSD left and CRUDE OIL right, real bid/ask/spread, cost estimate, 30s/1m diagnostics, active position, PAPER metrics, recent trades, and Day/Week/Month/Year net performance charts.
