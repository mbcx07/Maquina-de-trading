QUANTUM DUAL COMMODITIES R15 LINUX
Release: 2026.09.02-R15
Branch: feature/v34-dual-market-engine

FOCUS:
- Only XAUUSD and CRUDE OIL.
- Both are compared on Exchange and MT5.
- Exchange PAPER and MT5 PAPER start separate R15 sessions at $50 USD each.

SIGNALS R15:
- Replaces the overly strict all-or-nothing trigger with a visible confluence score.
- Live context: M1 EMA9/EMA21, RSI14, ATR14 plus 30-second microstructure and taker/tick flow.
- Default admission threshold: score >= 58.
- Spread/cost gate remains a hard veto: expected gross target must cover spread + fees + slippage by the configured edge multiple.
- Dashboard exposes LONG score, SHORT score, threshold, blockers and fulfilled confluences so WAIT is diagnosable.

CRUDE DIRECTION:
- Default BUY.
- User can switch CRUDE OIL to BUY only, SELL only or BOTH from the dashboard.
- The selected mode applies to CLUSDT/Aster and the broker crude symbol on MT5 simultaneously.

PAPER COMPARISON:
- Exchange R15 PAPER session starts at $50.
- MT5 R15 PAPER session starts at $50.
- Historical R14 rows are retained but do not affect the new R15 PAPER balances/statistics.
- Exchange still uses maximum leverage allowed by the contract when available.
- MT5 sizing is scaled to the $50 virtual account while respecting broker minimum/step size; broker bid/ask spread is included.

REALTIME CHART:
- Full candle/history payload refreshes periodically.
- A dedicated Server-Sent Events stream updates live bid/ask/spread every 500 ms by default.
- 30s micro-bar is merged into the chart without waiting for the full page refresh.
- Larger labels, higher contrast, live BUY/SELL/ENTRY/SL/TP markers and a visible stream status.

BACKTEST:
- In-app Exchange backtest supports 7D / 30D / 90D / MAX.
- MAX walks paginated 1-minute history backwards up to COMMODITY_BACKTEST_MAX_DAYS (default 365) or until the contract has no older data.
- Binance klines are paginated with up to 1500 candles per request.
- Historical result is explicitly labelled HISTORICAL_1M_APPROXIMATION because standard historical 30-second taker-flow candles are not available through the normal kline endpoint.
- Forward PAPER remains the exact 30s/1m comparison and is shown alongside historical WR, PF, return, DD and final balance.

VENUES:
- XAU Exchange: XAUUSDT on Binance USD-M Futures.
- CRUDE Exchange: CLUSDT on Aster / Binance Wallet.
- MT5 auto-detects XAU and crude symbols and uses live broker bid/ask spread.

SAFE WEB UPDATE:
- Quantum Update Agent remains host-side over a local Unix socket; Docker socket is not mounted into the web app.
- Updates are blocked while tracked positions are open.
